# INVESTIGATING THE MATERIALITY OF OIL SUPPLY IN THE FORMATION OF ITS PRICE

This repository contains the essay evaluating the macroeconomic drivers of global energy markets. The paper challenges the demand-led consensus by demonstrating how supply decisions, strategic capacity management, and information flows act as primary catalysts for market volatility and economic resilience.

## Abstract
This essay refutes the claim that supply decisions are immaterial in oil price formation by challenging the rigid boundaries of Kilian and Murphy's model through the lens of Baumeister and Hamilton's Bayesian framework, which attributes a primary and asymmetric role to supply shocks. By integrating Känzig's "supply news" theory, the analysis demonstrates how expectations and OPEC+ signaling influence financial markets well ahead of actual physical flows, serving as a vital barometer for the global economy. Utilizing Caldara and Iacoviello's GPR index as a benchmark, the text illustrates how the current abundance of non-OPEC supply acts as a shield against geopolitical crises, as evidenced by the 2026 Venezuela case study. Ultimately, while demand dictates the long-term trend, strategic supply management governs the turbulence and resilience of the global economic cycle.

## Key Theoretical Frameworks Covered
* **Physical Rigidities vs. Bayesian Flexibility:** A comparative analysis of the short-run oil supply elasticity bounds between Kilian & Murphy (2012) ($\leq 0.0258$) and the less restrictive Bayesian prior distributions of Baumeister & Hamilton (2019) (posterior median of $0.15$).
* **Information Flows and "Supply News":** Integration of Känzig’s (2021) High-Frequency Identification (HFI) framework, illustrating how OPEC+ announcements inject structural surprises into the futures curve before physical production shifts.
* **Geopolitical Risk Insulation:** Utilizing the Caldara & Iacoviello (2022) GPR Index as a baseline to analyze the "numbing effect" of modern non-OPEC supply buffers (US, Guyana, Norway, Brazil) during the 2026 Venezuela intervention.
